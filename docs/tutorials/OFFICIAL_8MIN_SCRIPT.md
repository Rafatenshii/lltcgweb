### Deliberate adaptations

- The video decides the first player with rock-paper-scissors; this app uses a coin flip,
  so `coin` keeps the video's meaning ("the winner decides") with the app's UI.
- The video narrates the hand exchange as three manual actions (set aside, draw, shuffle
  back). The app performs all three from one mulligan panel, so `mulligan` covers 1:04–1:35.
- Active / Energy / Draw Phases resolve automatically here, so `phase_active`,
  `phase_energy` and `phase_draw` narrate them as they happen instead of asking for input.
- Three board-tour steps have no video counterpart; the video shows a physical playmat,
  and players need the on-screen zone names before the coin flip.
- The `perf_intro` … `yell_blade` steps hold the opponent so the Performance does not start
  before the player has read the Performance Phase rules (`hold_cpu` in the guide).
